# 🎭 Oru Varthai (Tamil Charades)

A clean, modern, and high-energy Tamil Charades web application designed for parties, family gatherings, and fun! 

---

## 📖 How to Play

**Oru Varthai** (One Word) is a fast-paced game where you have to act out a Tamil Movie or Song title for your team to guess before the timer runs out.

### 1. Setup
*   Divide into two teams: **Team A** and **Team B**.
*   Decide which team starts first.

### 2. The Game Cycle
*   One player from the active team (the **Actor**) holds the device or faces it while others watch.
*   The Actor chooses a category: **🎬 Movies** or **🎵 Songs**.
*   As soon as the game starts, a 60-second timer begins.
*   The Actor must act out the word shown on the screen **without speaking, making sounds, or mouthing words**.

### 3. Scoring & Actions
*   **GOT IT (Swipe Right / Green Button):** If your team guesses correctly, hit "GOT IT" or swipe right to earn 1 point.
*   **PASS (Swipe Left / Red Button):** If the word is too difficult, hit "PASS" or swipe left to skip to the next word. No points are lost.
*   **Round End:** Once the 60 seconds are up, the device is passed to the next team.

---

## 🛠️ Features
- **Clean UI:** Monochromatic, high-contrast design for maximum readability.
- **Curated Content:** Over 100+ high-quality Movies and Songs that are actually "actable."
- **Swipe Support:** Fast, native-feeling gestures for seamless gameplay.
- **Phonetics & Descriptions:** Every word includes English phonetics and a brief description to help non-Tamil speakers join the fun.
